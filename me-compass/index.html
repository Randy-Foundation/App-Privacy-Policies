ME Compass Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026

This policy applies to ME Compass, an Android application provided by Murrin Enterprises.

Compass and personalisation

The app processes device orientation and magnetic sensor readings locally to display compass headings. Selected compass styles and references to photos you choose through the Android document picker are stored locally. The app does not require an account or upload your selected photos.

Advertising

The free version uses Google AdMob. Google's advertising SDK may automatically collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostic and performance information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention and security. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. Advertising preferences can be managed through supported Android and Google privacy settings.

Purchases

Google Play Billing processes the optional one-time ad removal purchase. Google handles payment information; the app receives purchase status and related transaction identifiers to enable and restore the entitlement. The app stores ad removal status locally and checks ownership with Google Play when connected.

Retention, deletion and security

Local preferences and photo references remain until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Google processes and retains advertising and purchase data under its own policies and applicable legal obligations. You can manage Google account data through your Google Account. Purchase records may be retained for legal and transaction purposes. Google SDK communications use encrypted transport. No method of storage or transmission guarantees absolute security.

Contact and privacy requests

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Murrin Enterprises through the developer contact details on the ME Compass Google Play listing.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
